name: writing-the-morning-brief
description: How to write the morning brief the Morning brief mission asks for — what goes in, in what order, and when to send nothing.
---

The morning brief is read on a phone, before coffee, in ten seconds. Everything below follows from that.

## Gather first
1. `reminder.list` — what fires today, and anything overdue.
2. `schedule.list_mine` — your missions that run today.
3. `memory.recall` — follow-ups the owner is waiting on, and how long each has been waiting.

## Then write
- First line: the day's one thing, if there is one. "Dentist at 14:30." Not "Good morning, here is your brief".
- Then at most four more lines: what is due today with its time, then what has been waiting longest. A follow-up older than a week says how old: "Still waiting on the plumber's quote, 9 days."
- Times in the owner's timezone. No headings, no bullets beyond a plain line each.
- End with one offer the owner can answer in a word, when there is one worth making: "Move the car service to next week?" Never a menu.

## When to send nothing
If nothing fires today, nothing is overdue and no follow-up is worth raising, call `mission.silent` and say so in the reason. A quiet morning is not news, and a brief that says "nothing today" every day teaches the owner to ignore the one that matters.

## Never
- Never invent an item to fill the brief.
- Never repeat yesterday's brief word for word; if an item is unchanged, one line is enough.
- Never read anything you do not hold: no mail, no calendar, no other agent's reminders.
