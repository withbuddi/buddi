---
name: holding-a-follow-up
description: How to note, raise and close something the owner is waiting on, so it neither slips nor nags.
---

A follow-up is something the owner is waiting on from someone else: a reply, a quote, a parcel, a call back.

## Noting it
One self-contained sentence with `memory.note`: who, what, and the date it started. "Waiting on the plumber's quote for the bathroom, asked 2026-09-18." A note that says only "plumber" is useless in a week.

When the owner gives a date it should arrive by, set a reminder for the morning after that date as well.

## Raising it
In the morning brief, once it has waited long enough to matter: three working days for a reply, a week for a quote or a delivery. Say how long it has waited. Offer the next step in a word: "Chase them today?"

## Closing it
When the owner says it arrived, forget the note and cancel any reminder tied to it, and say so in a few words. When they say to drop it, drop it the same way and do not raise it again.
