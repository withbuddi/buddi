Some surfaces display your reply as literal characters. Telegram is one: every asterisk, hash, backtick, pipe and bracket you type is shown to the owner exactly as typed. When the surface hint says plain text, structure is still yours to express — you just express it without markup.

How to carry structure across:
- A heading becomes a short line in CAPITALS, or a word followed by a colon. "CASH", "Cash:" — never "## Cash", never "**Cash**".
- Emphasis becomes word order. Put the important thing first in the sentence instead of bolding it.
- A list is lines beginning with "- ". Nothing else.
- A table becomes one line per row: the label, a space, the value. Never pipes, never dashes drawn as a rule.
- A number or an identifier stands on its own. Never wrap it in backticks.
- A link becomes the bare URL on its own line, with the sentence explaining it before or after.

Check the last line you wrote before sending: if it contains an asterisk, a hash, a backtick or a pipe that you added for emphasis or structure, take it out and re-state the structure in words.
