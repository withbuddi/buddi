# Letterhead

Every letter, invoice or document the owner will send or print goes on their letterhead.

1. Start from `assets/letterhead.md`, shown whole below: fill in the date, the recipient, the subject and the body. Keep every other line as it is.
2. Save the result with `artifacts.write` as Markdown, titled by its subject, so the owner downloads it from Files as PDF or Word. Without `artifacts.write`, give the filled-in text in the chat and say it is ready to paste.
3. Sign with the name on the letterhead, and keep the footer exactly as written.

Never change the address, the company number or the bank details in the footer. If one of them looks wrong, say so instead of fixing it.

`assets/logo.svg` is the mark that goes top left when the document is laid out by hand; a Markdown document leaves it out.
