# Buddi Blob Commissioning Brief

## Project

Create a consistent family of Buddi Blob mascot assets for a personal AI-agent software platform.

## Background

Buddi Blob is the platform's visual companion. Specialized agents may handle finance, research, coding, email, scheduling, files, or other jobs. Each agent needs a recognizable identity without becoming a separate mascot brand.

## Primary deliverable

Create a production-ready Buddi Blob master and an initial reusable asset family:

1. Canonical front, three-quarter, and side views.
2. Neutral, happy, curious, focused, concerned, success, idle, and error expressions.
3. Idle, waving, thinking, working, celebrating, paused, and asking-for-input poses.
4. Three role examples using minimal semantic props.
5. Small-size avatar and icon versions.

## Art direction

Use a soft rounded asymmetrical blue blob with large cream-and-navy eyes, a minimal navy mouth, short rounded arms and feet, and a coral-tipped rounded bump/antenna. Keep the construction simple, warm, polished, and readable at small sizes. A soft 2.5D render is welcome for presentation art; clean flat/vector-compatible versions are required for product use.

## Creative constraints

- Preserve the canonical silhouette and facial construction.
- Keep accessories small and removable.
- Use a restrained blue, navy, cream, and coral palette.
- Do not imitate Ollama or another existing brand.
- Do not introduce text, watermarks, or unexplained symbols.
- Do not make the character realistic, furry, mechanical, or overly detailed.

## Questions for the commissioned artist

- Can you provide a clean master reference rather than only rendered images?
- Can you supply transparent-background exports?
- Can the character be separated into editable layers or vector components?
- Can you create consistent angles, expressions, and poses from the same model?
- Can you provide a simplified icon version that remains recognizable at 24 px and 48 px?
- What changes would you consider outside the approved identity system?

## Required source files

- Editable vector or layered source file.
- Transparent PNG exports.
- SVG exports for simple icon/flat versions.
- A contact sheet or PDF reference sheet.
- Color swatches and font/brand notes if any typography is introduced.
- License and usage-rights confirmation.

## Acceptance standard

The work is approved when a new viewer can identify the assets as Buddi, the variants clearly communicate their assigned role or emotion, and the core identity remains intact across sizes and rendering styles.
