# Buddi Blob Character Bible

## 1. Purpose

Buddi Blob is the recognizable visual companion for Buddi's personal AI-agent software. It should feel intelligent, warm, calm, useful, and approachable. Different agents may have different jobs and personalities, but they should still look like members of the same Buddi family.

## 2. Core identity

### Canonical description

Buddi Blob is a soft, rounded blue companion with a compact asymmetrical body, large expressive cream-and-navy eyes, a small curved smile, short rounded side-arms, small feet, and a signature rounded bump/antenna tipped with coral.

### Personality

Buddi is a helpful companion rather than a superhero, executive, animal, or realistic robot. It is curious, capable, calm, friendly, and quietly expressive. Its intelligence should come from its behavior and tools, not from a complicated technical appearance.

## 3. Recognition anchors

These features define Buddi and should remain stable in almost every asset:

- Rounded asymmetrical blob silhouette.
- Bright blue primary body.
- Two large cream eye shapes with dark navy pupils and small highlights.
- Minimal navy smile or mouth.
- Small rounded arms integrated into the body.
- Two compact feet or lower body supports.
- One signature upper bump/antenna with a coral tip.
- Simple, uncluttered facial language.

At small sizes, the silhouette, eyes, and coral-tipped bump must still identify Buddi.

## 4. Visual style

The current concept direction is a polished soft-rendered mascot: clean geometric construction, rounded surfaces, gentle depth, soft studio light, and restrained glow. It may be presented as flat vector art for icons and UI, or as a soft 2.5D/3D render for marketing and character sheets.

Do not allow rendering effects to obscure the silhouette or make Buddi look like a realistic creature.

## 5. Color system

The following values are working references and may be tuned after the production master is approved:

| Role | Working color | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buddi blue | `#3F8EF7` | Main body and primary identity |
| Deep navy | `#152642` | Pupils, mouth, outlines, dark UI details |
| Warm cream | `#FFF7E8` | Eye whites and soft neutral surfaces |
| Buddi coral | `#FF786A` | Antenna tip and small action accents |
| Soft background | `#F7F4EF` | Presentation and light product contexts |

Color changes should normally be reserved for agent roles or status accents. The body should remain Buddi blue unless a deliberate campaign or accessibility variant is approved.

## 6. Fixed versus variable design

### Fixed identity layer

- Body silhouette and proportions.
- Eye construction and placement family.
- Mouth language.
- Coral-tipped signature bump.
- Rounded, soft construction.
- Overall friendliness and restraint.

### Variable persona layer

- Expression.
- Pose and gesture.
- Small tool or prop.
- Accent color.
- Optional hat, badge, headset, satchel, or simple role cue.
- Small differences in posture or energy.

### Restricted changes

These require a formal version bump and approval:

- Replacing the blob with a different body archetype.
- Removing or redesigning the signature bump.
- Changing the eye style substantially.
- Adding a permanent costume or large accessory.
- Changing the main body color system.
- Moving from friendly companion to a dramatically different personality.

## 7. Expression language

Approved baseline expressions:

- Neutral/helpful: relaxed eyes, small smile.
- Happy: curved closed eyes, open smile.
- Curious: widened eyes, small rounded mouth, slight head tilt.
- Focused: lowered brows or narrowed eyes, closed mouth.
- Concerned: raised inner brows, small downward mouth.
- Proud/successful: confident eyes, gentle smile, slight upward posture.
- Sleepy/idle: lowered eyelids, relaxed mouth.
- Error/caution: concerned eyes, small coral status accent; never panic or horror.

Expressions should remain readable with a few shapes. Avoid exaggerated cartoon distortion unless the asset is specifically an entertainment illustration.

## 8. Usage principles

Buddi can appear in product UI, agent cards, onboarding, empty states, status indicators, documentation, marketing, and social content. For UI, favor simplified icon versions. For editorial or promotional use, allow props and richer soft rendering while preserving the canonical identity.

## 9. Do not

- Copy Ollama or any existing mascot.
- Add random text, letters, or logos to the character.
- Use realistic fur, animal anatomy, or human anatomy.
- Make the character threatening, sarcastic, chaotic, or childish by default.
- Add excessive accessories that hide the body.
- Change the eyes or bump merely to make a new agent look different.
