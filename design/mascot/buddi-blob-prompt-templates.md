# Buddi Blob Prompt Templates

## A. Canonical master

Create an original Buddi Blob mascot for a personal AI-agent software platform. Use a soft rounded asymmetrical blue blob body, large expressive cream-and-navy eyes, a tiny navy smile, short rounded arms and feet, and one signature rounded bump/antenna tipped with coral. Use clean geometric construction, a friendly intelligent personality, restrained soft rendering, and a simple uncluttered presentation. Preserve the same silhouette and facial language across all views. No text, watermark, existing logos, realistic fur, complex anatomy, or busy background.

## B. New agent variant

Create a new Buddi Blob agent variant.

Role: `[AGENT ROLE]`

State/action: `[IDLE / LISTENING / WORKING / SUCCESS / ERROR / NEEDS INPUT]`
Role cue: `[ONE SIMPLE PROP OR SYMBOL]`
Accent color: `[COLOR]`

Keep the canonical Buddi Blob identity unchanged: blue rounded asymmetrical body, large cream-and-navy eyes, minimal navy mouth, rounded arms and feet, coral-tipped signature bump. Change only the expression, pose, one small role prop, and optional accent color. Make the result recognizable as Buddi at small size. No text, watermark, random symbols, excessive accessories, realistic fur, or copied logos.

## C. Expression sheet

Create a Buddi Blob expression sheet with the same character model in eight views: neutral/helpful, happy, curious, focused, concerned, proud/successful, sleepy/idle, and needs-input. Keep the exact same body silhouette, eye construction, coral-tipped bump, blue body, and simple face. Use a clean neutral background and no labels or text.

## D. Product icon

Create a simplified Buddi Blob avatar icon. Show only the head and upper body, with the rounded asymmetrical blue silhouette, large cream-and-navy eyes, minimal navy smile, and coral-tipped bump. Use high contrast, generous padding, no props, no text, no background clutter, and a design that remains recognizable at 24 px and 48 px.

## E. Marketing illustration

Create a polished soft-rendered Buddi Blob illustration for product marketing. Keep the canonical blue rounded blob, expressive cream-and-navy eyes, small smile, rounded arms and feet, and coral-tipped bump. Add only `[ONE SIMPLE SCENE OR TOOL]`. Use warm studio lighting, gentle depth, clean composition, and generous negative space. The character must remain more important than the environment. No text or watermark.

## Prompt review checklist

- Is the role explicit?
- Is the state or action explicit?
- Is the role cue limited to one simple prop?
- Are the fixed identity features repeated?
- Are prohibited changes listed?
- Is the intended output type clear: icon, flat asset, sheet, or marketing illustration?
