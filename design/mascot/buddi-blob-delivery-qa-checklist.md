# Buddi Blob Delivery and QA Checklist

## Identity review

- [ ] The silhouette reads as Buddi Blob without explanation.
- [ ] The body remains rounded and asymmetrical.
- [ ] The large cream-and-navy eyes match the approved family.
- [ ] The mouth remains simple and readable.
- [ ] The coral-tipped bump/antenna is present unless the asset is an approved close crop.
- [ ] Arms and feet remain soft and compact.

## Variation review

- [ ] The agent role is understandable from one clear cue.
- [ ] The expression or action is readable.
- [ ] Accessories do not hide the core body.
- [ ] Color changes are documented and intentional.
- [ ] The asset does not look like a separate mascot.

## Technical review

- [ ] Transparent PNG is supplied.
- [ ] SVG is supplied when the artwork is simple enough for vector output.
- [ ] Source file is editable and included.
- [ ] Large master export is included.
- [ ] Avatar/icon export is included.
- [ ] No accidental text, watermark, or background artifacts.
- [ ] Edges are clean at 100% and at small size.
- [ ] Contrast works on both light and dark UI surfaces.

## Naming and versioning

Use:

`buddi-blob__role__state__asset-type__vMAJOR.MINOR.ext`

Examples:

- `buddi-blob__core__neutral__master__v1.0.svg`
- `buddi-blob__research__working__avatar__v1.0.png`
- `buddi-blob__finance__success__illustration__v1.1.png`

## Approval levels

### Draft

Concept direction only. May contain temporary rendering or composition choices.

### Approved identity

Canonical body, face, colors, and signature bump are locked for production.

### Production asset

Approved identity plus required exports, source files, naming, and QA checks.

### Deprecated

Retained for history but not used for new work.

## Version rules

- Major version: body, face, color system, or signature feature changes.
- Minor version: new approved pose, expression, prop, or export treatment.
- Patch note: technical re-export with no visual change.

## Rights and records

- [ ] Artist or vendor identity recorded.
- [ ] License and commercial usage rights recorded.
- [ ] Source files received.
- [ ] Approval date recorded.
- [ ] Version and intended use recorded.
- [ ] Any exceptions to the Character Bible recorded.
